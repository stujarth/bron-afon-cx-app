Above & Beyond

What we added on top of the brief

For tenants

  • 🤖 AI diagnostic — photo/video upload, identifies issue, suggests fixes, books right trade
  • 💬 Floating Hafan AI — context-aware chatbot on every page, bilingual
  • 🏆 Rewards & gamification — points for engagement, badges, community draws
  • ♿ Accessibility toolbar — text size, high contrast, reduce motion (persisted)
  • 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Full Welsh language — every screen, every micro-copy

For staff

  • 📋 Unified casework queue — complaints, ASB, safeguarding, mutual exchange, adaptations
  • ⏱️ SLA tracking + risk chips on every case
  • 📞 Log on behalf of tenant — captures offline channels
  • 📅 Planned works management — programme builder with tenant-visibility toggle
  • 📊 Feedback dashboard — respond to ratings on completed works

Next

What we'd validate next

  • Real tenant testing — invite a small cohort to use it for a week and gather qualitative feedback against the same 30 stories
  • Integration spike — pick the highest-value integration (repairs system? rent ledger?) and prove the data flow end-to-end
  • Accessibility audit — formal WCAG 2.1 AA pass with NVDA + keyboard walkthrough
  • Welsh language review — native-speaker copy-check on the translations we've drafted
  • Branding pass — apply Bron Afon's house style (logo, colour, typography) once approved

The current prototype is a credible "v1 design" — not production code. Standing this up for real tenants is a separate, scoped build.
